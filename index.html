The real problem

The stakes

What miscommunication is costing you

It rarely looks like a communication problem. It looks like slow execution, friction between departments, or a leader who “isn’t working out.”

  • Time

    Decisions that won’t stay decided

    The same topic comes back meeting after meeting because people left with different versions of what was agreed.

  • Money

    Rework and dropped handoffs

    Teams build the wrong thing with total confidence. Deadlines slip and budgets stretch to cover the gap.

  • People

    New leaders who aren’t heard

    Talented leaders lose influence in their first months, then lose interest. Some of them leave.

  • Risk

    Global teammates who go quiet

    Across cultures, silence can mean agreement, respect, or real concern. Guess wrong and problems stay hidden until they’re expensive.

None of this is inevitable. It’s learnable.

What changes

A team that moves as one

Decisions that stick
